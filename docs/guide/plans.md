# Move plans

A move plan turns your captured layout into a sequence of practical physical changes. Stowplan shows why it made each suggestion so you can reject advice that does not match the room in front of you.

## Check readiness

Open **Plan** after the relevant spaces are mostly counted. The readiness panel separates information required to make a useful plan from details that would only improve confidence.

It may ask you to:

- finish an uncounted or in-progress space
- replace quick-capture defaults with a more useful item detail
- describe whether a destination is suitable
- add measurements when physical fit is uncertain

Each prompt opens the relevant Capture, Inventory, or Spaces editor. Measurements remain optional when capacity is not the question.

## Generate and review

Generate a plan and read the reasons on each suggested move. Stowplan considers:

- rules that make a destination unsuitable
- measured capacity when both sides have compatible measurements
- items that should stay together
- easier access for frequently used things
- physical distance
- whether moving one nested container avoids many item-by-item moves

Missing measurements are shown as unverified capacity, not as proof that an item fits or does not fit.

## Work through the steps

Complete plan steps in their displayed order. Later steps wait because an earlier move may free space needed by the next one.

Before completing a step, check the named source, destination, and amount against the physical item or container. If the saved workspace has changed since the plan was made, Stowplan stops and asks for review instead of moving a different record.

Use the item, container, and destination links to inspect the evidence without completing the step.

## Change your mind

Plans are suggestions, not instructions you must obey. Make a manual move, adjust the priorities, discard the plan, or generate a fresh one after substantial edits.

Completed plan steps are ordinary workspace changes. Review or undo them from **Activity** when the physical result differs from the plan.
